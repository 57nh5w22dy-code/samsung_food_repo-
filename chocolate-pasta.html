Chocolate Pasta

Silky chocolate pasta coated in a glossy dark chocolate sauce with just the right touch of sweetness.

Prep
10 minutes
Cook
10 minutes
Total
20 minutes
Servings
2 servings

Ingredients

  • 200 g dry chocolate pasta
  • 100 g dark chocolate (60–70%), chopped
  • ¼ cup (60 mL) 2% lactose-free milk
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Pinch of fine salt
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)

Optional toppings

  • Fresh raspberries or strawberries (optional)
  • Toasted hazelnuts or walnuts (optional)
  • Grated dark chocolate (optional)
  • Powdered sugar (optional)
  • Flaky sea salt (optional)
  • Vanilla ice cream (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta. Cook the chocolate pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 2–3 tbsp pasta water, then drain.
  2. Melt the chocolate. Place the chopped chocolate in a microwave-safe bowl. Melt using the microwave’s Chocolate Melt setting or 50% power in 20-second bursts, stirring until smooth.
  3. Heat the milk. Heat the milk in a separate microwave-safe cup until hot and steaming, about 30–45 seconds. Do not let it boil.
  4. Make the sauce. Slowly whisk the hot milk into the melted chocolate in 2–3 additions until smooth and glossy.
  5. Finish the sauce. Stir in the butter, salt, and vanilla until fully combined.
  6. Coat the pasta. Add the warm pasta and toss gently to coat. Use only enough sauce to lightly coat the pasta.
  7. Adjust the consistency. If the sauce is too thick, add warm milk or reserved pasta water, 1 tsp at a time, until silky.
  8. Serve. Twirl into bowls and add any desired optional toppings. Serve immediately.

Chef’s tip

Keep the sauce light so the pasta stays glossy—add liquid gradually. It should coat, not pool.

Make-ahead

Best enjoyed fresh. You can cook the pasta and make the sauce ahead; reheat gently with a splash of milk.

Storage & reheating

Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days. Reheat gently with a splash of milk.
